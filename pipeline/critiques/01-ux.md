# 01 UX critique (stage 5b, round 1)

Reviewed: 14 screenshots in pipeline/screens/, brief, IA, components.md, OPEN D-017 to D-022 (not re-reported). Severity: 🔴 blocks the portfolio or a top job, 🟠 visible craft or consistency gap, 🟡 polish. Owner tags: now / inbox / incident / escalation / foundation (shared components or shell).

Capture note (not ranked): `now-light-full.png` and `incident-sev1-light-full.png` are 1250px tall, identical to the viewport shots. The full-page capture is not full-page. The coverage bar, timeline and Resolve button are never shown. Fix the capture script (owner: foundation).

## Top-3 job costs

| Job | Today | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Take an escalation | Banner "Take over" then "Acknowledge" = 2 clicks. On Now, card Acknowledge = 1 click or `a`. | Fast, but the verb differs ("Take over" vs "Acknowledge") and `a` is ambiguous with 2 cards. |

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 🔴 Inbox table is clipped and its columns do not line up with the header (inbox)
Screens: inbox-light, inbox-team-light, inbox-dark-compact.
- Headers STATUS, OWNER and AGE (x≈766 / 1010 / 1190) sit over cells that never render. Titles run to the pane edge and are cut mid-word ("third-par", "3 tag", "FV-0..."). FV ids are truncated, which breaks the "never truncate IDs" rule in components.md.
- The Status marker, Owner and Age columns are invisible, so the triage job loses its three most useful signals.
- Fix: fixed-width columns (SEV 96, ID 88, Status 120, Owner 140, Age 64, all right-aligned numerics). The title cell flexes with `truncate` and a tooltip. Move the `FV-xxxx` and "3 tags" chips into their own column. Collapse the preview to a drawer at <1440 or let the table scroll horizontally with pinned SEV and ID.

### 2. 🔴 Preview pane is empty on load, wasting ~45% of the screen (inbox)
Screens: all inbox.
- "Select a row to preview it" floats mid-pane. components.md section 5 says nothing-selected shows "hint + top item to triage next".
- This is the hero interaction (tag to incident) and the default shot shows none of it.
- Fix: auto-focus the first row (SEV1 INC-4140) so the preview renders on load. If it is truly empty, show the next-to-triage card with its action keys. Re-shoot inbox with a row selected, a proposed SEV2? confirmed, and the bulk bar visible.

### 3. 🔴 The Incident page header is the heaviest chrome in the product and leaks builder copy (incident)
Screens: incident-sev1-*, incident-stale-light.
- The top-right text "Reachable from Inbox, Vehicle, Drive, Hazard, Shift, Cmd-K" is spec language rendered in the UI. A hiring manager reads it as unfinished.
- "Escalated" appears twice, as a chip (y≈255) and as a flag pill on its own row (y≈415).
- The header stacks six strata: breadcrumb, back link, chips, title, stepper, flag, meta. That is ~320px before any content. "Back to Inbox / Incident / INC-4140" is redundant.
- The Owner / Reported by / Opened / Last update / Due row floats right, detached from the title.
- Fix: delete the "Reachable from" line. Merge the flags into the chip row once. One breadcrumb only (Inbox / INC-4140). Put the meta in a 2-line definition list under the title. Target header ≤200px.

### 4. 🔴 Three overlapping "ownership" signals and a primary button that contradicts its own warning (incident)
Screens: incident-sev1-light, incident-stale-light.
- The header has Assign, Escalate and a primary "Move to Owned". The dashed callout below says nobody owns this and it cannot move past Triaged, then offers "Take ownership" and "Assign to...". That is three ways to assign.
- "Move to Owned" is the loudest button but is blocked by the callout's own precondition.
- The escalation card says "Current owner Priyanka" while the header says "Owner: No owner". Escalation owner and incident owner are never distinguished.
- Fix: when no owner, the header primary becomes "Take ownership" and "Move to Owned" is disabled with a reason tooltip. Delete the header Assign or the callout button. Label the card chain "Escalation owner" and the header "Incident owner".

### 5. 🔴 Shortcut keys are inconsistent across screens (foundation, incident, escalation, inbox)
- `a` means Acknowledge (Now, Escalation), assign (Inbox footer, IA, components.md) and Move to Owned (Incident header). Incident's Assign is `r`, rendered in a mono that reads as "ɪ".
- The spec says `e` escalate, `a` assign, `v` verify. The built product breaks its own vocabulary on the one surface that teaches it.
- On Now with 2 cards, `a` has no stated target.
- Fix: one key map. Incident header: Assign `a`, Move to Owned `o`... or `n` (next step), Escalate `e`. On Now, show the key only on the card that is actionable by you (focused card), and let `j/k` switch cards. Fix the `r` glyph or the font in `kbd`.

### 6. 🟠 SEV1 title and escalation state are shown 2 to 3 times per screen (foundation, now, escalation, incident)
- The banner shows the title in full (y≈93). The Now card then repeats it truncated ("Lidar sync lost mid-drive; planner fell…"). Escalation page shows banner, 32px H1, then a truncated card title.
- Incident shows banner, H1, and card.
- A SEV1 title should never be ellipsized. When the page is the card, the banner row for that same escalation should collapse (it already drops its button on escalation-rerouted).
- Fix: on Now and Escalation hide the global banner (or collapse the row for the current escalation to a 1-line "viewing"). Wrap card titles to 2 lines. On Incident, drop the embedded card's own title line (header already has it).

### 7. 🟠 Escalation cards: "Must act now" is on the wrong person and the strikethrough reads as an error (now, escalation)
Screens: now-*, escalation-rerouted-light.
- Right card: "Must act now" is highlighted on Priyanka (not you) and the 04:12 timer is as large as the one you must act on. Two cards of equal weight = the user cannot tell which is theirs.
- Left card: the timed-out owner is in bold strikethrough ("Marisol Quintanilla-Veyrat de la Cruz"). It looks like a deleted record, and on the takeover page it truncates to "…de la C…".
- "until no one left on the rota" is the scariest line in the product and is set in 12px grey.
- Fix: mark the actionable row "You" and give your card the strong border and fill. De-emphasise the other (no big timer, 20px). Replace strikethrough with muted name plus "Timed out 14m ago" chip. Rewrite the line "If unanswered, nobody is left to page".

### 8. 🟠 Verb mismatch: "Take over" (banner) vs "Acknowledge" (card) vs "Open" (banner, other card) (foundation, escalation)
Same Escalated-and-must-act state, three labels. The banner "Take over" lands on a page where the first action is a different word. Fix: banner button = "Acknowledge" with `a` hint that acks in place, or "Review" if it navigates. "Open" for the not-yours escalation is fine but should read "View".

### 9. 🟠 Map in Incident looks fake and hides the thing it is for (incident)
- No marker is selected. There are two near-identical SEV1 triangles and 8 identical grey hazard squares, while the card says "No hazard linked". The legend text sits on top of the route. "12 markers shown" is cut off by the card edge. Blank white rectangles read as missing tiles.
- Fix: draw the incident's marker larger with the 2px `focus` ring and a label; mute the other markers to 40%. Move the legend out of the map. Hazard squares should not render if "No hazard linked", or the copy should change. Fix the clipped caption.

### 10. 🟠 Now: the differentiator (coverage) is below the fold; the tile for it omits the key fact (now)
- The escalation block eats 520px. At 1440x900 only the tile row is visible, and the coverage bar starts at y≈1200 and is cut off.
- "Uncovered gaps: 1" says nothing about when. Principle 6 ("absence must be drawn") is not drawn above the fold.
- The tile also wears a SEV2 chip. A coverage gap is not an incident, so this contradicts "severity is only for tags/incidents" (principle 1) and "don't use sev colors for decoration".
- Fix: add a one-line gap text to the tile ("Uncovered 02:00-06:00 · night shift"), drop the SEV chip (use the hatch and the violet flag). Shrink the escalation cards (compact row variant once acknowledged) or put a mini coverage strip at the top right of the title row.

### 11. 🟠 Debt tiles: uneven rhythm and a composite hero (now)
- "UNCOVERED GAPS" wraps to 2 lines, pushing its value 22px lower than the other three. Rows 1 to 3 have dead space between the delta and the link.
- Only the hero has a sparkline. The link label "14-day total, oldest to newest" describes the sparkline rather than the destination.
- The chevron means "up" but not good or bad. Rising debt looks neutral.
- The hero 48 is a sum of footage, incidents, stale escalations and gaps. It reconciles (23+23+1+1), but it is a composite, which components.md tile "Don't" warns against.
- Fix: single-line labels ("Uncovered gaps" → "Gaps"). Align values on one baseline. Words in the delta ("worse than last shift"). Link the hero to "Everything behind 48 (4 queues)". Consider showing the arithmetic under the number: "23 + 23 + 1 + 1".

### 12. 🟠 Inbox chrome and severity noise before the first row (inbox)
- Three rows of chrome (saved-view tabs, Mine/Team + filter, hint strip) sit above the header, ~125px, and the hint strip is repeated in the footer with different keys. `t` (triaged), `d` (dismiss) and `Enter` appear in neither place completely.
- 8 of 9 SEV2 rows wear identical dashed "SEV2?" chips. The proposed state is correct by spec, but at this volume it drowns the 2 confirmed rows (the signal).
- Footer "Escalated 2" is an orphan flag pill with no label; it reads as a filter or a legend.
- The table touches the sidebar with no gutter, so severity edge bars sit on the nav border (Now and Incident use a ~22px gutter).
- Fix: merge the hint strip into one place (footer). Put saved views and scope on one row. Add a "Proposed" column label, or show `?` only on the glyph and keep the fill. Label the footer "2 escalated". Give the table a 22px gutter or an intentional edge.

### 13. 🟠 Escalation takeover: two equal-weight primary buttons and a resolve action at the fold edge (escalation)
- "Acknowledge" and "Mark escalation resolved" are both full ink-blue. Resolve is disabled (D-020) but in dark it looks enabled, with no reason shown (conventions: disabled needs a tooltip). It sits at y≈1223, half outside the fold.
- Each protocol row shows both a checkbox and an "Open" word on the right (redundant, 11px). No back link to Now.
- Fix: Resolve uses the secondary style until it is enabled, with an inline reason ("Acknowledge and tick 5 steps"). Replace "Open" with the tick time. Add "Back to Now". Put the progress "0 of 5" next to Resolve too.

### 14. 🟡 Shell: nav and top bar carry unexplained elements (foundation)
- The nav badges mix work counts (Inbox 20, Review 23) and non-work counts (Fleet 9, Plan 3). "Now 3" is unexplained. Fleet and Plan need no badge unless it is a state.
- "Graveyard" is jargon for the night shift, and the hatched icon has no legend. The AM/PM/Graveyard control looks the same as Inbox's Mine/Team but means something different (scope of the whole app vs a filter).
- The unlabelled hamburger next to the theme toggle is the density switch (it shows a blue fill in the dark compact shot). It needs a label or tooltip with "Compact".
- Pinned views show no counts, no icons, and the same names also appear as tabs in Inbox.
- "Ctrl K" is shown on what looks like a Mac target. Show ⌘K by platform.
- Fix: badges only where "something needs you". Rename to "Night". Give pinned views a count. Label the density toggle.

### 15. 🟡 Gallery shows seams a hiring manager will spot (foundation)
Screen: gallery-light.
- "Loading Loading" (duplicate), an empty DATA HEALTH tile with only a chip, "FAILING 1" with two SEV1 chips, a stray duplicate "SEV2" chip, and ~200px of grey below the coverage bar.
- Fix: fix the loading copy, give DATA HEALTH a value and state word, show one chip, trim the page. Better: do not put the gallery in the portfolio set; use the real screens.

## Strengths worth protecting (do not "fix")
- Principle 3 works. The SEV1 triangle, SEV2 diamond and SEV3 ring survive greyscale, and the dark theme holds hierarchy.
- The 04:12 countdown at 40px mono is legible at arm's length, and the "backup next" chain shows who pages next.
- The on-call rota panel ("No one has to remember") is the best single idea on screen.
- The escalation protocol checklist is clear, orderly, and calm. Dark and light are equally well-tuned.

## Portfolio hero shot
**Strongest: `escalation-active-light.png`.** It carries the brief's core claim (escalation never depends on one person) in one frame: chain, countdown, backup, rota and ordered protocol, with calm hierarchy and clean type.

Make it stronger:
1. Show an acknowledged, half-done state (2 of 5 ticked, timer stopped, "Acknowledged by Devika 15:43") so the frame shows a decision, not a to-do.
2. Remove the repeated title (banner, H1 and truncated card) and hide the banner row for this escalation.
3. Make Acknowledge the single dominant action and demote Resolve to secondary-disabled with a reason.
4. Keep the fold at 1440x900 clean. The Resolve button is clipped today.

Runner-up: `now-light.png`, but only after fixes 7 and 10 put the coverage bar above the fold. That frame carries the IA story (workflow-first, debt visible).
